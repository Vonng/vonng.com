**Martin Kleppmann** is an associate professor at the University of Cambridge. His research focuses on local-first collaboration software, distributed systems, and security protocols. He is also one of the people behind Automerge.

He wrote Designing Data-Intensive Applications (DDIA), with Chris Riccomini joining as co-author for the second edition. Ruohang Feng maintains a Chinese translation project for the book.
