**Steven Vaughan-Nichols** (Steven J. Vaughan-Nichols, often known as SJVN) is a technology and business journalist and editor of Practical Technology. He has written extensively for ZDNet, Computerworld, and other publications.

His reporting focuses on Linux, operating systems, networking, open source software, and the businesses behind them. Before becoming a journalist, he worked as a programmer and network and email administrator.
