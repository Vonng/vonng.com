**John De Goes** (John A. De Goes) is a software engineer, architect, entrepreneur, and technical author. He created the Scala functional programming library ZIO and is CEO of Ziverge.

His work spans functional programming, distributed systems, and developer tools. He shares software design ideas through writing, talks, and training. This blog includes a Chinese translation of his essay on MongoDB’s analytics capabilities and partner ecosystem.
