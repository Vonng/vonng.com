**Stephan Schmidt** founded Amazing CTO, where he coaches CTOs, engineering leaders, and founders. His background includes software development, engineering management, and entrepreneurship, with experience at eBay and ImmobilienScout24.

He writes about team building, technical debt, architecture, and engineering leadership, connecting technical decisions to business needs. This blog includes a Chinese translation of his essay recommending PostgreSQL.
