**DHH** created Ruby on Rails and co-owns 37signals, the company behind Basecamp and HEY. He develops open source software and has co-authored several books on work and business with Jason Fried.

On HEY World, he writes about software development, running a business, and technical infrastructure, including 37signals’ move from public cloud services to its own hardware.
