**alitrack**, also known as Steven Lee, is a data engineer and open source developer working on DuckDB, PostgreSQL, data tooling, and Windows support.

He created **duckdb_fdw**, a PostgreSQL foreign data wrapper that integrates DuckDB and its analytical capabilities. The extension is included in Pigsty. His other projects cover DuckDB extensions, data pipelines, and SQL-based analysis tools.
