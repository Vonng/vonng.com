**Paul Copplestone** is the co-founder and CEO of Supabase, an open source application platform built around PostgreSQL. Supabase combines databases with authentication, storage, and realtime services.

He participates in the open source community and writes about PostgreSQL, developer tools, and building companies, with an emphasis on making database infrastructure accessible to application developers.
