**Laurenz Albe** is a senior consultant and support engineer at CYBERTEC. He has worked with and contributed to PostgreSQL since 2006, written core patches, and created **oracle_fdw**, the Oracle foreign data wrapper.

His technical writing covers SQL, performance, administration, and migration, with a focus on understanding and resolving practical production database problems.
