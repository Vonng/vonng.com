**Hannes Mühleisen** is a co-creator of DuckDB and a researcher in analytical databases and data management. He developed DuckDB together with Mark Raasveldt.

His work explores embedded analytics, efficient query execution, and ways to reduce the deployment and data movement costs of analytical systems. This blog includes a Chinese translation of his essay on small data and infrastructure complexity.
