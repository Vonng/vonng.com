**Rui Gong** is the director of media technology at MediaStorm (影视飓风), working on the technical infrastructure behind film and video production.

His article on MediaStorm’s DaVinci Resolve database describes the team’s use of PostgreSQL and Pigsty for project libraries, covering high availability, backup and recovery, access control, and database operations.
