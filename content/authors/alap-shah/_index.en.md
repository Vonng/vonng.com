**Alap Shah** is an investor and entrepreneur who co-founded LOTUS Investment Management, Littlebird.ai, Thistle, and Sentieo. He writes about how AI affects businesses, employment, and public markets.

He co-authored The 2028 Global Intelligence Crisis with Citrini Research, exploring the economic consequences of rapid AI adoption through a hypothetical scenario.
