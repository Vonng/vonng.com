**Ajay Kulkarni** is the co-founder and CEO of Tiger Data, formerly Timescale. He founded the company with Mike Freedman to build data infrastructure on PostgreSQL.

The team began with an IoT platform and developed TimescaleDB to handle machine data at scale. Its work spans PostgreSQL extensions, time-series workloads, and managed database services.
