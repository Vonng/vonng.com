**Andy Pavlo** is a tenured associate professor of computer science at Carnegie Mellon University and leads research at ClickHouse Labs. His research covers autonomous database systems, transaction processing, and large-scale analytics.

He co-founded the database tuning company OtterTune and teaches database systems at CMU. His courses, research projects, and annual database industry reviews connect system design with practical operation.
