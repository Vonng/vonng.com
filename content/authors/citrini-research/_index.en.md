**Citrini Research** is an independent publication covering global macroeconomics, thematic investing, and industry trends. Its research connects macro conditions, sector changes, and company fundamentals to examine how technology reshapes markets and business models.

This blog includes a Chinese translation of its essay The 2028 Global Intelligence Crisis, co-authored with Alap Shah. The essay uses a fictional future memo to explore an economic scenario rather than make a forecast.
