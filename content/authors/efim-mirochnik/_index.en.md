**Efim Mirochnik** works in IT infrastructure, operations, and information security. He has written for the Ahrefs engineering blog about data centers and infrastructure economics.

His essay How Ahrefs Saved US$400M in 3 Years by NOT Going to the Cloud compares colocation and public cloud costs using Ahrefs’ hardware, storage, network, and facility expenses. A Chinese translation appears on this blog.
