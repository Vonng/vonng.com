**digoal**, also known as De Ge, is a PostgreSQL technical writer and community contributor. His public writing covers SQL, performance tuning, database administration, extensions, and practical applications.

He maintains **digoal/blog**, a regularly updated collection of Chinese technical articles, and writes about open source, AI, and the database industry. This blog includes his discussion of Pigsty’s license change.
