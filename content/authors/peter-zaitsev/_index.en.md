**Peter Zaitsev** founded Percona after leading the High Performance Group at MySQL. His technical background spans database internals, computer hardware, performance optimization, and application scaling.

He writes about database operations, software licensing, and open source business models, following developments across MySQL, PostgreSQL, and other open source data systems.
