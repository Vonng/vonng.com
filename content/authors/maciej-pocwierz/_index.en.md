**Maciej Pocwierz** (Maciej Poćwierz) is a software engineer with experience across several industries. He also built Scrabblecam, a tool that recognizes physical Scrabble boards from photographs.

In 2024, he published How an empty S3 bucket can make your AWS bill explode, documenting his investigation into unexpected charges caused by external requests to a private S3 bucket. A Chinese translation appears on this blog.
