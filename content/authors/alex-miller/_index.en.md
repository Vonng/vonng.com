**Alex Miller** is a database engineer, a FoundationDB contributor, and the author of Transactional. He writes about transaction processing, data replication, storage, and distributed databases.

His essays and talks connect research papers with implementation details, examining the trade-offs between consistency, failure recovery, and performance.
